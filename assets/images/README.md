# Image assets

Website photography is stored locally and grouped by use:

- `profile/`: `clinician-stock.png` is the supplied profile artwork used on Home, About, and Gallery. The folder also contains the supplied event portrait and unused illustrative clinician photos.
- `care/`: patient conversation and follow-up imagery used on Home, Contact, and Appointment Inquiry.
- `neurology/`: brain imaging used for educational neurology content.
- `clinical/`: consultation and care-team imagery used for service information and the gallery.
- `gallery/`: additional consultation imagery.

The supplied `profile/clinician-stock.png` is a square profile graphic with embedded text and Methodist Hospital branding. It is displayed with `object-fit: contain` so the content is not cropped. The branding in the artwork does not independently verify an affiliation. Other healthcare photographs are illustrative stock images and do not depict Dr. James Jack, his patients, his office, or a verified hospital. Keep these distinctions clear when using or replacing the images.

The supplied promotional/profile graphics in the parent `images` folder include a physician portrait and Methodist Hospital branding. They are not used on the website because neither the physician identity nor the hospital affiliation has been verified. Do not publish them as factual endorsements or credentials without verification and authorization.

Before production use, verify image usage rights and any attribution requirements. Keep meaningful alt text, and do not add a real street address, phone number, credentials, or institutional affiliation until confirmed by official sources.
